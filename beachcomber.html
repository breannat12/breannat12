Beachcomber

A field notebook for logging what you find on a walk — photo, tide, place, and a guess at the species.

Swift · SwiftUI · Core ML · Offline-first

The story

An excuse to learn SwiftUI properly, and to stop losing the notes I was keeping in my camera roll. Offline-first, because the beaches worth walking rarely have signal.

Highlights

  • Offline-first with conflict-free sync on reconnect
  • Auto-tags each find with tide state and location
  • On-device species suggestions from a small Core ML model
